# Worked example

*Made up lead, made up company. This shows the shape of the output, not real research.*

**Input:** Asha Menon, IT Manager, Northwind Health (northwind-health.example), Bengaluru. Source: Contact Sales form. Keyword: "kandji competitors".

**Step 0, signal.** Contact Sales plus a competitor keyword for an Apple device management tool. She is comparing options now. High intent.

**Step 1, identify.** IT Manager for two years. Northwind Health is a HealthTech company of about 120 people, Series B funded last year.

**Step 2, fit.** Direct buyer. Growth stage segment (50 to 200 people). Strong fit: regulated industry, recent funding, hiring.

**Step 3, stack and triggers.** Their open IT Admin role asks for experience with Kandji and Google Workspace, supporting a mixed Mac and Windows fleet, and "helping drive SOC 2 Type II". So: Apple only device tool, Windows machines it can't cover, and an audit coming.

**Step 4, voice.** A recent post from Asha: "Scaling IT at a healthcare startup means compliance can't be an afterthought."

**Step 5, synthesis.**

**Hook:** Kandji covers their Macs but not their Windows machines, right as a SOC 2 Type II audit begins. That split is exactly where audit gaps appear.
